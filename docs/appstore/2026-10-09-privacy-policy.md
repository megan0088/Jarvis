# AppleMind — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

AppleMind collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking of any kind. This page explains what that means in practice.

## The app cannot reach the internet

AppleMind is sandboxed and ships with exactly two entitlements: the App Sandbox itself, and
read/write access to files **you** pick in an open panel. It has **no network entitlement** —
outgoing and incoming connections are disabled in the build. The app is not able to send your
data anywhere, because it is not able to make a network connection at all.

## What stays on your Mac

Everything the app remembers lives in its own container on your Mac:

- **Conversations** with Apl, including the model's own transcript.
- **Reminders** you create, and the local notifications scheduled for them.
- **Your nickname** and app settings.
- **Files and folders you point the app at**, and any backups it makes before writing to
  them. Backups are stored inside the app's container, never in your folders.
- **Images** you create with Image Playground.

None of it is uploaded, synced, or shared. There is no iCloud sync and no server.

## Apple Intelligence

Chat answers come from Apple's on-device foundation model, which runs locally on your Mac.
Image generation uses Apple's Image Playground, also on-device. Your prompts are handled by
macOS, not by us, and never reach a server belonging to this app's developer. Apple's own
privacy terms cover those system features.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Erase All Data** removes everything the app stores: conversations, the model's
transcript, reminders and their pending notifications, your nickname, images, and backups.
Deleting the app removes its container along with all of it.

## During beta testing

If you install AppleMind through TestFlight, **Apple** collects crash logs and basic usage
data under Apple's own policy, and may share crash reports with the developer. That is
TestFlight's behaviour, not the app's — the app itself still collects and sends nothing.

## Children

AppleMind is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
